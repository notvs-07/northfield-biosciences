# northfield-biosciences
NorthField Biosciences is an AI-powered livestock healthcare and management platform designed to reduce preventable losses among India's smallholder farmers.

NORTHFIELD BIOSCIENCES
PROJECT REFERENCES
===================

This file lists sources used for the livestock-sector context, statistics,
and existing digital/animal-health infrastructure referenced in the project.

1. DEPARTMENT OF ANIMAL HUSBANDRY AND DAIRYING (DAHD) — ANIMAL HUSBANDRY STATISTICS
   Government of India.
   https://dahd.gov.in/en/schemes/programmes/animal-husbandry-statistics
   Use: Official livestock census publications, breed reports, and animal
   husbandry statistics.

2. 20TH LIVESTOCK CENSUS — LIVESTOCK POPULATION DATA (2019)
   Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of India.
   https://www.dahd.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/09/792e283baf05487202e9710656ffb0df.pdf
   Use: Species-wise livestock population. The 2019 census recorded
   approximately 536.76 million livestock.

3. BASIC ANIMAL HUSBANDRY STATISTICS 2025
   Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of India.
   https://www.dahd.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/02/71836f721eb692711897d31f382eb926.pdf
   Use: Livestock-sector contribution and Gross Value Added (GVA).
   The report gives livestock GVA of approximately Rs. 15.06 lakh crore
   for 2023–24 at current prices.

4. PRESS INFORMATION BUREAU — LIVESTOCK CENSUS
   Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of India.
   https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1813802
   Use: Government summary of the 20th Livestock Census and its coverage.

5. BHARAT PASHUDHAN / NATIONAL DIGITAL LIVESTOCK MISSION
   Official Bharat Pashudhan dashboard.
   https://bharatpashudhan.ndlm.co.in/
   Use: Reference for India's existing digital livestock identification
   and information infrastructure. NorthField Biosciences is proposed
   as a complementary service layer, not a replacement.

6. PRESS INFORMATION BUREAU — DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION OF THE DAIRY SECTOR
   Government of India.
   https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2212724
   Use: Background on digital platforms and traceability initiatives
   across India's dairy ecosystem.

7. LIVESTOCK HEALTH AND DISEASE CONTROL PROGRAMME
   Press Information Bureau, Government of India.
   https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1887058
   Use: Background on government livestock health and disease-control
   programmes.

IMPORTANT NOTES
---------------
- Census figures refer to the 20th Livestock Census (2019); do not present
  them as a current 2026 animal count.
- The Rs. 15.06 lakh crore figure is livestock GVA for 2023–24 at current
  prices, as reported in Basic Animal Husbandry Statistics 2025.
- NorthField Biosciences' AI triage, animal profiles, reminders, claims
  support, and service-network features are proposed product capabilities,
  not claims that these services are already deployed or clinically validated.
- AI-generated risk flags should support, not replace, qualified
  veterinary assessment. The platform is not intended to independently
  diagnose or prescribe treatment.
- Verify every statistic against the latest official publication before
  using it in a public pitch, report, or investor document.
